Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to Gamy for Android (com.gamy.game)

The short version

Gamy collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection in the app. Your best score and settings are saved on your own device and are never sent anywhere. Nobody — including the developer — can see how you play.

1. Who this policy is from

Gamy is an independent game developed by Gamy Team. This policy explains what happens to information when you install and play Gamy on an Android device. It applies to the app and to this page, and nothing else.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information. Gamy does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal data, and it has no feature that could send such data off your device.

Specifically, the app does not:

  • create an account or ask you to sign in;
  • use analytics, crash reporting, attribution, or advertising SDKs;
  • show ads or use an advertising identifier;
  • make purchases or process payments;
  • transmit gameplay, scores, device information or identifiers to any server.

3. Information stored on your device

Gamy remembers a small amount of gameplay information so it can show your progress the next time you open it. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your best score, best combo, total runs and total motes collected;
  • your sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is not linked to you or to any identifier. You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset progress inside the app, or by uninstalling Gamy. Android may include app data in a device backup if you have backups enabled in your system settings; that backup is controlled by you and your Google account, not by Gamy.

4. Permissions

Gamy requests only what it needs to play sound and give tactile feedback. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, files, or photos.

  • Audio playback — to play the bundled sound effects and music. No audio is recorded.
  • Vibration — for optional haptics, which you can turn off in Settings.

5. Third parties

Gamy contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The app is distributed through Google Play; Google's own handling of information related to downloading and installing apps is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. That relationship is between you and Google, and Gamy does not receive any personal information from it.

6. Children's privacy

Gamy is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no personal information about a child for us to hold, delete, or disclose.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer information to third parties, because we do not receive any. There is no data broker, no marketing partner and no server-side profile of your play.

8. Security

Your scores and settings stay in the app's private storage, protected by the Android app sandbox. Since no data is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept. Keep your device lock enabled and your system updated — that protects the local data on your phone better than anything an app can do.

9. Changes to this policy

If Gamy ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if a future version adds online leaderboards — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The current version of this policy always applies to the current version of the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about Gamy's privacy practices can be sent to obinduka716@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.
